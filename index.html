Part evolution enthusiast, part computer nerd and part molecular wizard-in-training, I am a cross-trained biologist interested in fundamental questions about how bacterial genomes evolve. What dictates the organization of bacterial genomes? How does recombination shape how bacteria evolve? How do we define bacterial species, and how can new ones evolve? I am interested in these questions and more. I find that complex evolutionary questions are best tackled with a combination of evolution experiments, genetics, comparative genomics, and theory.

Currently, I am a PhD candidate at the Barrick Lab at Michigan State University. I have been here since 2025, and I am affiliated with the Microbiology, Genetics and Immunology and Ecology, Evolution and Behaviour graduate programs.
